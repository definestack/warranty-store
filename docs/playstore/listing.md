## Title

Warranty Store

## Short Description

Track warranties, store invoices, and never miss an expiry date.

## Full Description

Warranty Store is a privacy-first app for tracking product warranties and invoice receipts — fully offline.

Add a purchased item once — its purchase date, warranty length, price, and store — and Warranty Store calculates when its cover expires, reminds you before it does, and keeps the invoice attached so you never have to dig through drawers or photo galleries again.

KEY FEATURES

- Warranty tracking: record purchase date, warranty length, price, store, and notes for every item.
- Extended warranty support: layer one or more extended warranty periods — provider, duration, and cost — on top of the original manufacturer cover.
- Invoice & document storage: attach invoice photos from your camera or gallery, or PDF documents, for both the original purchase and any extended warranty.
- Expiry reminders: local notifications 30 days before, 7 days before, and on the day a warranty expires.
- Search & filter: see items at a glance by status — active, expiring soon, or expired.
- Local backup & restore: export every item and its documents into a single file and restore it later; importing merges safely so nothing already on your device is overwritten.
- Dark mode & multiple languages: light, dark, or system theme, with English, Spanish, French, and German support.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account is required. No data leaves your device. There is no analytics in this release. Invoice images and documents stay in the app's private storage.

Warranty Store works completely offline, start to finish.
